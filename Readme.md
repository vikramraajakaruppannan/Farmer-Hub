Farmer-Hub
An AI-powered smart agriculture platform is developed to address the major challenges faced by modern farming systems. The platform enables early crop disease detection using advanced machine learning models, helping farmers take preventive action before significant crop loss occurs. It provides expert advisory services, allowing farmers to receive timely guidance on crop management, soil health, and irrigation practices. A farmer collaboration module facilitates knowledge sharing, experience exchange, and community-driven problem solving. The system also offers direct market access, connecting farmers with bulk buyers to ensure fair pricing and transparent transactions. An investor–farmer land leasing module enables efficient utilization of underused agricultural and urban land by connecting investors with farmers. Real-time environmental and market data analytics support informed decision-making. The platform integrates artificial intelligence with cloud-based technologies to ensure scalability and reliability. By reducing dependency on intermediaries and manual practices, the system enhances operational efficiency. Overall, the platform promotes sustainable agriculture, higher productivity, and improved economic stability for farming communities.

TECH STACK:
Frontend: React.js – for building a responsive and user-friendly web interface
Backend: FastAPI – for high-performance RESTful API services
Database & Backend Services: Supabase (PostgreSQL) – for secure data storage, authentication, and real-time synchronization
Artificial Intelligence / Machine Learning: Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch – for crop disease detection and predictive analytics
Cloud & Deployment: Supabase Cloud / API hosting services for scalability and reliability

IMPACTS OF FARMER HUB:
Enables early detection of crop diseases, significantly reducing crop loss and improving yield quality
Provides timely expert advisory, helping farmers adopt scientific and data-driven farming practices
Enhances farmer-to-farmer collaboration, promoting knowledge sharing and community-driven solutions
Facilitates direct farmer-to-buyer market access, ensuring fair pricing, transparency, and reduced dependency on intermediaries
Supports investor–farmer land leasing, enabling efficient utilization of underused agricultural and urban land
Improves resource efficiency through real-time analytics, reducing water, fertilizer, and pesticide wastage
Encourages sustainable and precision agriculture, contributing to long-term environmental conservation
Strengthens economic stability for farmers by improving productivity, profitability, and market reach.

ABSTRACT:
Modern agriculture continues to face significant challenges such as crop diseases, inefficient resource utilization, lack of timely expert guidance, and limited access to transparent markets. Traditional farming practices and isolated digital solutions often fail to provide integrated decision support for farmers. This paper presents an AI-powered smart agriculture platform that combines early crop disease detection, real-time environmental analytics, expert advisory services, farmer collaboration, direct farmer-to-buyer market access, and investor–farmer land leasing within a unified system. Convolutional Neural Networks are employed to identify crop diseases from leaf images at early stages, while data analytics support informed decision-making and market forecasting. The platform is developed using React.js, FastAPI, and Supabase, ensuring scalability, security, and real-time data handling. Experimental results demonstrate improved disease detection accuracy, faster advisory response, enhanced collaboration, and increased market transparency. The proposed system offers a comprehensive, sustainable, and scalable solution for precision farming and modern agricultural ecosystems.
